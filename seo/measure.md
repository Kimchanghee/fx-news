- Numeric baseline: pending console data (GSC/Bing exports are owner-held). Values are unknown, not zero.
- Record per run: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position by group (fx, commodities, guides, tools), compared over equal windows excluding the latest 2-3 days.

- No ranking, traffic or revenue guarantees. Ads remain off.
